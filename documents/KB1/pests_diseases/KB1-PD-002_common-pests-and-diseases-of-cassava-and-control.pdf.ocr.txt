Cassava Mealybug The mealybug (Phenacxcus spp.) is a small, pinkish, wingless insect covered with white fluffy wax. It attacks the tips, stems, and leaves of cassava plants by sucking the sap from the plant. It causes two types of damage; or direct damage caused by their sucking fæding habits, and indirect damage by the build-up of the mty mold on the leaf surface due to mealybug excrement.
