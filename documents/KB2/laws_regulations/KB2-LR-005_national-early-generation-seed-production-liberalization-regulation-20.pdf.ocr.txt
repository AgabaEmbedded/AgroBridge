The objective of these Regulations is to liberalise the production of Early Generation Seeds "EGS" in Nigeria by enabling qualified private sector actors, plant breeders, and seed scientists, to engage in the production of breeder and foundation seeds, for the purpose of increasmg the availability, accessibility, and quality' of EGS to support a robust and competitive seed industry.
