EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM This Act repeals the National Agricultural Seeds Act, Cap. N5, Laws of the Federation of Nigeria, 2004 and establishes the National Agricultural Seeds Council to promote and stimulate the development ofdependable seed industry, regulate and control the registration of released varieties, protect the fanner from the sales ofpoor quality seeds, facilitate the production and marketing of high quality seeds in Nigeria, and provide legal backing for official testing certification, sales, importation, exportation and use of seed. This Act also, promotes greater private sector participation in the seed subsector in line with the current agricultural seed policy globalisation and export trade promotion.
